Privacy Policy

ReelKit Publisher · Last updated: 21 October 2026

ReelKit Publisher is a private desktop tool used only by its operator with his own accounts. It has no other users.

Data the app accesses

  • TikTok: basic profile (display name, open ID) via user.info.basic, to show which account is connected; and permission to upload the operator's own videos to his TikTok inbox as drafts via video.upload.
  • YouTube / Facebook: permission to upload the operator's own videos to his own channel / Page.

Where data is stored

Access tokens are stored only on the operator's own computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. Nothing is stored on any server run by the app, and no data is sent anywhere except to the official APIs of TikTok, Google and Meta to perform the uploads.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its stored tokens. Access can also be revoked at any time in TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions), Google Account permissions, or Facebook Business Integrations.
