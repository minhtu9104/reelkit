ReelKit Publisher

A private desktop tool used by one independent creator to publish his own videos.

ReelKit Publisher runs on the creator's own computer. It prepares short educational chalkboard videos made by the creator and sends them to his own social media accounts (TikTok, YouTube, Facebook Page) so he can review and publish them.

What it does on TikTok

  • Signs in with Login Kit so the creator can connect his own TikTok account.
  • Uploads a video the creator made to his own TikTok inbox as a draft (Content Posting API, video.upload). Nothing is published automatically — the creator opens TikTok, edits the caption and posts it himself.
  • Reads basic profile information (user.info.basic) only to show which account is connected.

The app is not offered to the public and has no other users.
